Who I Am & What I Do

Hi! I'm Julen Rosado, a Systems and Network Technician. I've always been drawn to technology and love focusing on solving everyday real-world problems—from setting up and properly configuring a local network to troubleshooting why a PC won't boot or why a system is running slow.

I have hands-on experience building PCs, managing users and permissions in Windows Server (Active Directory), configuring Linux environments, and assisting users whenever they need technical support.

Technical Skills (Hard Skills)
  • Networking & Configuration

    Configuring TCP/IP, routers, switches, VLANs, DHCP/DNS, and cabling so the network runs smoothly without interruptions.

  • Operating Systems & Servers

    Day-to-day experience with Windows 10/11, basic administration of Windows Server (Active Directory, GPOs), and Linux distributions (Ubuntu/Debian).

  • Hardware & Troubleshooting

    Building PCs from scratch, diagnosing hardware failures, replacing components, and general IT maintenance.

  • Virtualization & Backups

    Using VirtualBox and VMware for lab testing, disk cloning, and simple backup strategies.

Personal Skills (Soft Skills)
  • Logical Problem Solving

    Curiosity and patience to go step-by-step until finding the root cause of a hardware or network issue.

  • Teamwork & Collaboration

    I adapt well to working with teammates, sharing tasks, and learning from experienced colleagues.

  • Eagerness to Learn

    Constant drive to discover new tools, commands, and more efficient ways of working.

  • Clear & Approachable Communication

    Direct interaction with users to understand their issues and explain solutions clearly without unnecessary jargon.
